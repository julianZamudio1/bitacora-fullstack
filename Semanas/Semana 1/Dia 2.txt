Conclusiones:
+---------------------------+------+-------+--------+-------------------------------+
|URL de inicio              |Saltos|Códigos|Location|URL final                      |
+---------------------------+------+-------+--------+-------------------------------+
|http://sustayta.com.mx     |   0  |   200 |   -    |la misma                       |
+---------------------------+------+-------+--------+-------------------------------+
|http://www.sustayta.com.mx |   0  |   200 |   -    |la misma                       |
+---------------------------+------+-------+--------+-------------------------------+
|https://www.sustayta.com.mx|   0  |   200 |   -    |la misma                       |
+---------------------------+------+-------+--------+-------------------------------+
|https://sustayta.com.mx    |   0  |   200 |   -    |la misma(deberia ser la única) |
+---------------------------+------+-------+--------+-------------------------------+

agregar en el <head>:
<link rel="canonical" href="https://sustayta.com.mx">
